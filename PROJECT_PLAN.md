# Compost Science Lab — Project Plan & Tracking

## Domain: compostculture.com (or worldofcompost.com)
**Angle:** Data-driven, scientific approach to composting. Not "how-to" opinions — real measurements, comparisons, and evidence-based recommendations. Think Wirecutter meets a lab notebook.

---

## Vision

A site that answers *which* composting method works best for *specific conditions*, backed by temperature logs, decomposition rates, carbon/nitrogen ratios, and real-world testing data. Affiliate links to thermometers, moisture meters, specialized bins, and scientific-grade tools.

**Different from worldcompost.com:**
- worldcompost.com = opinion-based guides, personal experience, YouTube companion content
- compostculture.com = comparative studies, lab-style testing, product benchmarking with real metrics
