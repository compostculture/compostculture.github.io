About

Why this site exists

Composting advice is everywhere. Measured composting advice is not. Compost Culture exists to close that gap: every product recommendation starts from published specs, test data, and long-term user reports — and every one of them carries a clear verdict on who it's for and who should skip it.

How we evaluate

The bar for a product on this site is the same every time:

  • Published specs first. Dimensions, materials, capacities, and manufacturer-published tolerances — from the listing and the manufacturer, not from marketing copy.
  • Independent data second. Where tests exist — thermal response, construction reviews, long-term owner reports from forums and review bases — we read them and cite what we use.
  • Honest tradeoffs always. Every pick names the situation where it's the wrong choice. A recommendation without a caveat is an ad.
  • No invented numbers. If a figure can't be sourced, it's flagged as a rule of thumb or left out. "Real data, not opinions" is the whole brand.

Who's behind it

Compost Culture is run by Newell, the person behind the @worldcomposting YouTube channel, where the long-form composting work has lived since 2026. The site is the companion to that channel: the videos show the process, the site carries the specs, the comparisons, and the gear list in one place.
