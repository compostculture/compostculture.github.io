## How it works

1. Each product has a **key** (e.g. `reotemp-20`). Pages reference the key, never the URL.
2. `js/site.js` resolves key → URL and **appends `?tag=worldcomposti-20` automatically**.
3. You can paste either:
   - a **plain product URL** (`https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002P5RGMI`) — the tag is added for you, or
   - an **already-tagged URL** (e.g. from your Associates dashboard) — it is used as-is.
4. To update: paste the tagged URL into the **Tagged URL** column below (or send it to me — I'll wire it into `config/affiliate.js` in one line).
