Live worms

Any bin is dead without the crew. We source live worms from Hungry Worms, a dedicated worm farm in Franklin, Idaho that ships with a live-arrival guarantee — when you're ordering live animals, buying from the farm rather than a marketplace is the whole game. Live animals ship with conditions: check the guarantee and shipping window before you order.

  1. 01
    Red wigglers, 1/2 lb (live) — Hungry Worms

    The standard starter load for a single bin: a half pound of red wigglers, shipped live from a dedicated worm farm with a live-arrival guarantee.

    View red wigglers at Hungry Wormsaffiliate link
  2. 02
    European nightcrawlers, 1/2 lb (175+) — Hungry Worms

    The hardy option: 3–6-inch worms that tolerate a wide temperature range, double as fishing bait and livestock feed, and ask for under 20 minutes a week. Orders scale to 10 lb (3,500+).

    View European nightcrawlers at Hungry Wormsaffiliate link
  3. 03
    African nightcrawlers (per lb, ~300 worms) — Hungry Worms

    The fastest workers: pound for pound they eat 1.5–3× faster than red wigglers. The catch is the band — 70–80°F ideal, below 50°F lethal — so they're for heated garages, greenhouses, and warm-climate bins.

    View African nightcrawlers at Hungry Wormsaffiliate link
