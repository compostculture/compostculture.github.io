Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site collects no personal data from you. It runs one anonymized analytics counter, uses affiliate links (Amazon, Hungry Worms, GEME Bio), and sets no marketing cookies. Last updated: October 2026.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count pageviews. We have enabled IP anonymization. Analytics cookies are used to understand which pages are read; they are not used to build a profile of you or to target you with advertising, and we do not share individual visit data with anyone.

Affiliate links

Compost Culture participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon. When you click an affiliate link on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We also participate in partner programs with Hungry Worms (live worms) and GEME Bio (the Terra 2) on the same no-cost terms. Amazon may set its own cookies on your browser when you land on its pages; Amazon's cookie policy describes what they collect. Full disclosure on the About page.

What we do not do

  • No account system, no sign-up, no email collection.
  • No advertising networks beyond our affiliate partners (Amazon, Hungry Worms, GEME Bio).
  • No sale or sharing of any visitor data.
  • No tracking of you across other websites.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them. There is no data collection mechanism on the site to collect it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Material changes (e.g., adding new tracking or data collection) will be noted here in plain language.
